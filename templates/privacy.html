Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core features of Spendly, such as displaying your expense history, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and authenticating your account. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your information will be permanently removed from our systems.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting and email delivery services, to operate the application. These providers only process data as necessary to support Spendly's functionality and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
